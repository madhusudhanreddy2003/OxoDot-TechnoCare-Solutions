Who We Are

OXODOT TECHNOCARE SOLUTIONS is a trusted IT partner delivering innovative, scalable, and secure technology solutions across industries. We combine expertise, agility, and innovation to transform businesses into digital leaders. OxoDot TechnoCare Solutions is an IT company in INDIA, dedicated to providing technology-driven solutions for hospitals, health systems, Telecommunication and Real Estate groups. It specializes in offering end-to-end IT services, including software development, cloud solutions, cybersecurity, and IT consulting. Additionally, the company provides business process outsourcing and information technology services to local Government Departments and NGOs within India. Also to few Clients of US, UK, MiddleEast. OxoDot also offers a wide range of services, including CFO advisory, environmental and social governance services, financial planning and analysis, enterprise risk and compliance services, human capital management, catering to various industries to healthcare, aviation, automotive, public services, retail, technology, AI-ML and agriculture sercives/ development/ RND.
